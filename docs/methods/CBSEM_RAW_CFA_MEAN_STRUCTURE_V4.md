# CB-SEM Raw CFA Mean Structure V4

## Status

This is an Internal/Experimental Labs scientific engine slice. It is not a
Standard capability, is not release-qualified, creates no qualification
receipt, and does not change CapabilityRegistryV2 coverage or evidence state.

## Executable boundary

The V4 path executes joint normal-theory maximum-likelihood estimation of the
covariance and mean structures for raw, continuous, single-group CFA models.
The scientific `SemModelV4` parameter table must explicitly contain exactly
one observed-intercept row for every measured indicator.

Factor locations may use either:

- `FactorMeanPolicyV4::FixedZero`, with no latent-mean parameter row; or
- `FactorMeanPolicyV4::Estimated`, with marker-loading identification, one
  matching free latent-mean row, and a fixed intercept for the marker
  indicator.

Observed intercepts and latent means preserve fixed/free status, finite open
bounds, starts, and within-family equality labels. Stable SemModelV4 parameter
IDs are retained in analytical results, the canonical result document, and
schema-6 saved results.

The objective is the existing covariance ML discrepancy plus

```text
(observed_mean - implied_mean)' * implied_covariance^-1
    * (observed_mean - implied_mean)
```

Both terms use the same listwise-complete raw rows. Expected-information
standard errors combine covariance and mean derivatives. Failed
identification, non-positive-definite matrices, singular information, invalid
bounds/starts, and nonconvergence remain typed failures.

| Layer | Mean-free path | Raw CFA mean path |

| Exact estimator | `cbsem_ml_exact_parameter_table_v3` | `cbsem_ml_exact_parameter_table_v4` |
| Moment adapter | exact V3 identity | `cbsem_ml_compiled_moment_input_v4` |
| Moment result schema | 2 | 3 |
| Recipe runner adapter | `compiled_recipe_v4_cbsem_plan_v2_execution_v2` | `compiled_recipe_v4_cbsem_plan_v2_execution_v3` |

Mean-free raw, covariance, and scaled-correlation execution remains on the V3
identity with empty, serialization-omitted mean payloads.

## Canonical and persistence contract

V4 canonical results add:

- the exact observed-mean digest to the estimation summary;
- observed, implied, and residual mean tables; and
- explicit V4 estimator, moment, result-schema, and runner-adapter cells.

The schema-6 append and read boundaries reject mixed V3/V4 identities,
missing V4 mean tables, unexpected V3 mean tables, or an absent V4
observed-mean digest.

## Independent oracle work evidence

The validation-only
[product fixture](../../validation/fixtures/cbsem_raw_cfa_mean_structure_v4_product_fixture.json)
is generated by an explicitly enabled test through the live Recipe-v4 CB-SEM
runner. A normal runner regression requires that checked-in fixture to equal a
fresh product result exactly. The fixture binds the estimator, moment adapter,
moment-result schema, and runner-adapter identities rather than copying those
identities into a validation script after execution.

The transparent
[NumPy/SciPy oracle](../../validation/cbsem_raw_cfa_mean_structure_v4_oracle.py)
independently reconstructs the preregistered raw observations and verifies:

- the raw-input digest, sample size, variable order, observed means, and ML
  covariance;
- all 11 parameter estimates, stable parameter IDs, fixed/free status, and
  expected-information standard errors;
- implied means and the joint covariance-plus-mean objective; and
- `converged=true` with the product gradient inside its declared `1e-5`
  objective-stagnation convergence envelope.

The comparator rejects unknown fields, non-finite diagnostics, duplicate or
incomplete parameter and implied-mean identities, incorrect fixed flags,
invalid standard errors, identity or input tampering, and gradients outside
the convergence contract. Parameter and implied-mean row order may change
because comparison is bound by stable identity rather than list position.

The checked
[work report](../../validation/results/cbsem_raw_cfa_mean_structure_v4_oracle_work_report.json)
records that the independent self-checks and this product-fixture comparison
pass. This closes one product-to-transparent-oracle microcase only. The case is
a just-identified, one-factor, three-indicator CFA and is not evidence for the
full supported CFA mean-structure shape space. It creates no qualification
receipt and does not change coverage or evidence state.

Remaining qualification blockers are a second independently maintained SEM
implementation, qualification-sized bias/coverage/failure simulations,
broader identified CFA shapes, archive/export and packaged-Windows evidence,
performance and soak evidence, and independent scientific review. NumPy,
SciPy, and Ruff are also not yet pinned by the validation requirements or
wired as a required CI gate.

## Explicit blockers

The following remain unsupported and fail before estimation or result
commitment:

- covariance/correlation mean vectors;
- structural regressions, structural intercepts, and general SEM latent means;
- effects-coding, fixed-variance, or reference-group identification for an
  estimated latent mean;
- ordinal thresholds, transformed/structural observed variables, groups,
  weights, clustering, strata, and non-listwise missing-data policies;
- derived location parameters and cross-family equality labels; and
- metadata-only CB-SEM Special Assumptions. Every implied covariance or fixed
  variance must first become an explicit stable parameter-table row.

General SEM means require a separately contracted structural location model
such as `mu = (I - B)^-1 alpha`; this V4 CFA slice does not silently infer it.
