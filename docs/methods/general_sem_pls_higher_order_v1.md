# General SEM PLS Higher-Order Constructs V1

This method contract covers the two bounded Standard cells for General SEM
higher-order constructs:

- `qpls3.pls.general_sem_higher_order_point`
- `qpls3.pls.general_sem_higher_order_full_model_case_bootstrap`

The complete scientific scope, supported approach/HCM matrix, exclusions,
estimands, persistence rules, and product behavior are defined in
[`GENERAL_SEM_PLS_HIGHER_ORDER_V1.md`](../GENERAL_SEM_PLS_HIGHER_ORDER_V1.md).

Both cells support one non-nested second-order HOC in a raw, listwise,
standardized, path-weighted, single-group recursive PLS model. Unsupported
models are blocked without changing the authored model. Historical HOC cells
and projects retain their original identities.
